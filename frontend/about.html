Manifesto

Job boards optimize for recruiter attention, not your time. We believe remote work discovery should be deterministic and inspectable: every score is arithmetic over eight weighted dimensions, every listing carries its source, and nothing is fabricated to look busy.

No opaque ranking. No invented metrics. If a number is shown, it comes from the live catalog.

Research & method

The ranking engine scores each opportunity across remote availability, worldwide eligibility, disclosed salary, beginner friendliness, AI-work relevance, English requirement, application simplicity, and freshness. Weights are configurable and must sum to 1.0.

Eligibility and skill matching use a transparent keyword taxonomy — the same rules, every time, with no learned black box in the product path.
