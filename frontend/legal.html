Policies

Privacy, terms & security

DEDAN Remote only collects publicly available listings. Your account data is scoped to you and never sold or used to train models.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what the service needs to function: an email and password (stored as a secure hash), and the preferences, saved roles, and applications you create. Opportunity data is fetched from public job boards and stored locally.

  • We do not sell your data or share it with recruiters.
  • We do not index or publish your resume or profile.
  • Session tokens live only in your browser's local storage.
  • You can delete saved roles and applications at any time.

Terms of Service

By using DEDAN Remote you agree to use it lawfully and to respect the terms of service of each platform we monitor. The service aggregates public listings for your personal job-search use.

  • Listings are provided as-is; verify details before applying.
  • Do not abuse the service or attempt to disrupt scraping targets.
  • Compliance with each platform's terms remains your responsibility.

Security Disclosures

We take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

  • Passwords are hashed; database queries are parameterized.
  • Secrets are held in environment configuration, never in code.
  • Report vulnerabilities privately via the repository's security advisory channel.
